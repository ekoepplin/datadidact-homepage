Hi, I'm Eugene. Welcome to my personal blog.

Pretty much everything here began life on my to-read list, that bottomless pile of tabs and bookmarks labeled "later." Writing about a topic is my way of finally getting to it and sorting out my thoughts. Sharing it is a bonus: if it was interesting enough to bookmark, maybe it's interesting to you too.
